# The Golden Ticket Trials

**A book by Walusimbi Leon (SGSS)**

## About

Five people from five countries each receive a golden ticket to Blackmoor Manor, where every challenge they survive doubles their personal pot — starting at a million and compounding with each round. But the tickets were rigged: only one can leave with the full vault, and the others leave with nothing but the memory of what they betrayed. Alliances form in the dining room and shatter in the maze. The manor always collects its due.
